I'm a cybersecurity professional with years of experience in SOC operations, threat detection, and incident response — spanning military cyber defense, national healthcare infrastructure, and enterprise-level environments.

My career has focused on:

- Designing detection content and improving SIEM/EDR effectiveness across platforms like Splunk, Defender for Endpoint, and Sysmon
- Driving secure architecture and cyber threat resilience during the NHS’s COVID-19 response
- Mentoring and managing SOC teams, ensuring operational maturity and KPI-driven performance

With a strong foundation in secure software engineering (Python, Django, SQL), I build tools that bridge red and blue team needs — from phishing analysis and malware sandboxing to detection engineering and automated response.
